# Project 5: Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Objective

Analyze retail sales data and generate business insights by studying sales, profit, regional performance, category performance, product performance, and sales trends.

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## Tasks Completed

- Imported the Superstore Sales dataset using Pandas.
- Checked the dataset structure and data types.
- Checked missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- Calculated sales and profit statistics.
- Performed filtering and sorting.
- Analyzed region-wise sales and profit.
- Analyzed category-wise sales and profit.
- Used groupby() for business analysis.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends.
- Identified top-performing products based on sales.
- Calculated overall profit margin.
- Created multiple visualizations.
- Prepared an executive summary and business recommendations.

## Visualizations

- Region-wise Sales — Bar Chart
- Category-wise Sales — Bar Chart
- Monthly Sales Trend — Line Chart
- Sales Distribution — Histogram
- Sales vs Profit — Scatter Plot
- Region-wise Profit — Bar Chart
- Category-wise Profit — Bar Chart
- Top 10 Products by Sales — Bar Chart

## Key Business Insights

- Region-wise analysis helps identify areas with strong sales and profitability.
- Category-wise analysis helps compare the performance of different product categories.
- Monthly sales trends help understand changes in business performance over time.
- Top-selling products can be identified using total sales.
- Comparing sales and profit helps identify areas with lower profitability.
- Profit margin provides an overview of overall business performance.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on regions that generate higher sales and profit.
- Review low-profit regions and categories for improvement opportunities.
- Promote high-performing products to increase revenue.
- Monitor products with high sales but low profit margins.
- Use monthly sales trends for better inventory and sales planning.
- Optimize discounts carefully to maintain profitability.
- Regularly monitor sales and profit KPIs for data-driven decisions.

The Retail Sales Analysis project successfully analyzed sales, profit, regional performance, category performance, product performance, and sales trends using the Superstore dataset.
